## Stakeholder Tracking Template

Copy this structure into Notion or Airtable. Track every person you contact.

```
Contact Name: [First, Last]
Title: [Specific title — apprenticeship coordinator, not "HR"]
Organization: [Union local, technical college, etc.]
Email: [Direct email]
Phone: [Direct phone]
Pain Point Hypothesis: [What problem do they solve?]
First Contact Date: [YYYY-MM-DD]
Meeting Scheduled: [Date + time, or "no" if declined]
Meeting Completed: [Y/N + notes from meeting]
Interest Level: [Hot (wants pilot), Warm (interested), Cold (not now), No]
Next Step: [What's the next move?]
Follow-up Date: [When to check in again?]
Notes: [Any key quotes, objections, or insights]
```

Use this to never lose track of a conversation or opportunity.
